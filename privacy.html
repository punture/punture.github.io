Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who uses this app

PX13 Mail is used only by its owner, on their own laptop, with their own Google account. It is not available to other people.

What Google data it accesses

  • Read Gmail (gmail.readonly): to search and read messages so the assistant can summarise them for the owner.
  • Compose Gmail (gmail.compose): to create reply drafts, and to send a draft only after the owner has approved that send.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email content is processed on the owner's laptop by a locally hosted language model. It is not sent to any external AI service or other third party.
  • Summaries and drafts are kept on the owner's laptop and in their own Gmail account. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's laptop. The owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

PX13 Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
