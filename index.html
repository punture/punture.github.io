PX13 Mail

PX13 Mail is a private, single-user email assistant. It runs on its owner's own laptop and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission, it reads their Gmail inbox to summarise messages and prepare reply drafts. Every email it sends is sent only after the owner has reviewed the draft and approved that specific send. It does not delete mail, change labels or settings, or share mail with anyone.

The assistant runs on a language model hosted on the same laptop. Email content is not sent to any external AI service.
